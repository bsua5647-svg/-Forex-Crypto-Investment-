## Withdraw

A withdrawal refers to moving funds out of a trading account, exchange, or investment platform to an external wallet or bank account.

Key considerations:
- Verify wallet or bank account details before initiating a withdrawal.
- Check platform withdrawal fees, processing times, and limits.
- Be aware of security steps such as 2FA, KYC verification, and anti-fraud checks.
- Review market conditions and risk exposure before taking profits or exiting positions.
- Maintain records for tax, compliance, and account reconciliation.

This section supports safe and informed capital management for forex, crypto, and broader investment activities.
